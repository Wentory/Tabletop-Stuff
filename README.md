# Tabletop Stuff

## What is Tabletop Stuff?

**Tabletop Stuff brings tabletop games into your Minecraft world.** Sit down with friends, deal cards by hand, and watch everyone play around the same table.

## Games

### NULL (UNO-inspired game)

> **2–4 at the table** · **Multiplayer or solo with up to 3 bots**

Match the top card by color or symbol and try to empty your hand first. Down to one card? Call **LAST!**

Planned additions include games played with a standard deck of cards, chess, checkers, backgammon, a Monopoly-inspired board game, dice games, and tools for tabletop RPGs like D&D.

## How to get the games

1. Craft a **Games Workshop**.
2. Open its catalog and select a game.
3. Gather the listed materials and craft the game in the workshop.
